🦭 Seal Slide - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026
Developer: Loaviel Games

1. Introduction

Loaviel Games develops Seal Slide, a mobile endless runner game. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the game.

2. Information We Collect

The current version does not require an account or ask players to provide personal information. Loaviel Games does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information through the game's current gameplay features.

3. Locally Stored Data

Seal Slide saves limited gameplay information, including your best score, on your device using Unity PlayerPrefs. This information is used to remember your progress between sessions. It is not currently uploaded to an online leaderboard.

4. Third-Party Services

The current version does not intentionally integrate advertising, analytics, online accounts or third-party leaderboard services. Seal Slide is distributed through Google Play, which operates under Google's own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing

Loaviel Games does not currently share locally stored gameplay information with external parties through the game's gameplay features.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay information remains on your device until cleared or removed. You can generally delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Seal Slide. This may reset your saved high score.

7. Children's Privacy

Seal Slide does not require account registration or personal information to play. Loaviel Games does not knowingly collect children's personal information through its current gameplay features.

8. Future Features

Future versions may introduce optional online features, including leaderboards. If these change how information is processed or shared, this policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the game develops. Changes will be published on this page, with the revision date updated.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact Loaviel Games at loaviel.games@gmail.com .
